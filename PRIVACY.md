# Privacy Policy — Mainstream

*Last updated: 18 October 2026*

Mainstream (listed on the App Store as "Mainstream: Transfer Data") moves data between two phones as an animated code: one phone shows it on its screen, the other reads it with its camera.

## What we collect

Nothing. Mainstream has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive, store or see anything you send or receive.

The app's only internet use is Apple's own: when a received location is shown on a map, Apple Maps loads the map for that area (covered by Apple's privacy policy). Links in received items open only when you tap them, in the app that handles them.

## How your data moves

What you send is encoded on your device and shown on your screen; the receiving phone decodes it on that device. Nothing passes through the internet or through us.

Because the transfer is visual, anyone who can see or film your screen while it is sending could receive the same data. Send private material only where that isn't a concern.

## Permissions and what they are used for

Each permission is requested only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

- **Camera:** reads codes shown on another phone, and takes photos you choose to send. Camera frames are processed on the device as they arrive and are not saved or sent anywhere.
- **Microphone:** records audio clips you choose to send. Recordings stay on your device until you send them.
- **Photos:** the system photo picker gives the app only the photos and videos you pick. Received photos and videos are saved to your library only when you tap Save; the app asks for add-only access and cannot read your library.
- **Contacts:** the system contact picker gives the app only the contact you pick. A received contact is added only if you confirm it in the system's contact card.
- **Location:** read once, only when you choose to send your current location, and included in what you send. It is not stored or tracked.
- **Wi-Fi:** when you choose to join a network from a received Wi-Fi code, the app asks iOS to join that network.

## Data stored on your device

Received items are kept in temporary storage while they are open and are deleted when you close them, unless you save or share them. Photos you choose to send can be re-encoded to a smaller size first; location metadata is removed in the process. Your settings (such as density and speed) are stored on your device.

## Share extension

When you share an item into Mainstream from another app, it is shown as a code on your screen in the same way. It is not stored or sent anywhere else.

## Children

Mainstream does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
