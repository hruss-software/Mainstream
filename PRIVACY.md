# Mainstream privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Mainstream (on the App Store as "Mainstream: Transfer Data") moves data between two phones as an animated code. One phone shows the code on its screen and the other reads it with its camera.

## What we collect

Nothing. Mainstream has no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising or tracking, and we never receive or see anything you send or receive.

The only time the app touches the internet is when it shows a received location on a map: Apple Maps then loads the map for that area, under Apple's privacy policy. Links in received items open only if you tap them, in whichever app handles them.

## How your data moves

What you send is encoded on your phone and shown on your screen. The receiving phone decodes it on that phone. Nothing passes through the internet or through us.

Because the transfer is visual, anyone who can see or film your screen while it's sending could receive the same data. Keep that in mind before sending anything private.

## Permissions

You can turn any of these off at any time in Settings.

- Camera: reads codes from another phone and takes photos you choose to send. Receiving is the app's main screen, so it asks for the camera when you first open it. Frames are decoded as they arrive and are never saved or sent anywhere.
- Microphone: records audio clips you choose to send, and is only asked for when you start a recording. Recordings stay on your phone unless you send them.
- Photos: the system photo picker gives the app only the photos and videos you pick. Received photos and videos are saved to your library only when you tap save; the app asks for add-only access and can't read your library.
- Contacts: the system contact picker gives the app only the contact you pick. A received contact is added only if you confirm it on the system contact card.
- Location: read once, only when you choose to send your current location, and sent as part of that item. It isn't stored or tracked.
- Wi-Fi: when you choose to join a network from a received Wi-Fi code, the app asks iOS to join it.

## What stays on your phone

Received items are kept in temporary storage while they're open and deleted when you close them, unless you save or share them. Photos and videos you send can be re-encoded to a smaller size first, which also removes location metadata from photos. Settings such as density and speed are stored on your phone.

When you share something into Mainstream from another app, it's shown as a code on your screen in the same way and isn't stored or sent anywhere else.

## Children

Mainstream doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
